Overview

At Vaibhav Celebrations, we strive to ensure your complete satisfaction with our products and services. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the terms under which cancellations, returns, and refunds are processed.

This policy applies to all purchases made through our website vaibhavcelebrations.in, including celebration packages, physical products, personalized items, and digital deliverables.

1. General Principles

  • All refund requests must be made through our official support channels.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method used at checkout.
  • Processing time for refunds is 7-10 business days after approval.
  • Bank processing may take an additional 3-7 days for the amount to reflect in your account.
  • Refund eligibility depends on the type of purchase and timing of the request.

2.2 Cancellation After Dispatch

  • Once the order is shipped, cancellation is not guaranteed.
  • If the shipment is intercepted before delivery, a refund minus shipping costs will be processed.
  • If delivery is attempted and refused, return-to-origin (RTO) charges apply.

  • Hygiene-sensitive products

  • Items without original packaging

3. Personalized / Made-to-Order Products

Items customized with names, photos, or specific designs

3.1 Before Production Begins

StageCancellationRefund
Order placed, no design work startedPermittedFull refund
Design shared, awaiting approvalPermittedFull refund minus design fee (if applicable)

3.2 After Production Begins

  • Production started after approval: Cancellation restricted. No refund unless defective.
  • Reason: Personalized items cannot be resold and materials are committed.

3.3 Defective Personalized Items

If the delivered item is:

  • Materially different from approved design
  • Has printing/production defects
  • Damaged during transit

Report within 48 hours with clear photos. We will provide replacement or full refund.

3.4 Not Eligible for Return

  • Items that match the approved design (minor variations are expected)
  • Errors in customer-provided information (names, dates, photos)
  • Subjective design preferences ("not what I imagined")

5.2 Rescheduling

  • Free rescheduling: If requested more than 30 days before the event (subject to availability).
  • Rescheduling fee: May apply for requests within 30 days due to vendor rebooking costs.
  • Limit: One free reschedule per booking; subsequent changes may incur fees.
  • New date: Must be within 6 months of original date.

5.3 Cancellation by Vaibhav Celebrations

In rare cases where we must cancel (vendor unavailability, unforeseen circumstances):

  • Full refund will be provided, OR
  • Alternative arrangement of equal or greater value offered

5.4 Partial Execution

If the event is partially executed due to issues beyond our control:

  • Refund will be calculated proportionally based on services not delivered
  • Documented evidence of non-delivery is required

6. Force Majeure

If event execution is prevented by circumstances beyond reasonable control:

  • Natural disasters, pandemics, government restrictions
  • Venue closure, curfews, strikes
  • Extreme weather making execution unsafe

Resolution Options:

  • Rescheduling: To a mutually agreed date within 6 months
  • Credit: Full value as store credit valid for 12 months
  • Partial refund: Amount minus non-recoverable costs incurred

Each case is handled fairly with transparency about costs already committed.

7. Free Consultation / Enquiry

  • Submitting a consultation or enquiry form is free and does not create a paid booking.
  • No payment is collected; therefore, no refund is applicable.
  • You are under no obligation to proceed after a consultation.
