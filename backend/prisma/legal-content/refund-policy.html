Overview

At Vaibhav Celebrations, we strive to ensure your complete satisfaction with our products and services. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the terms under which cancellations, returns, and refunds are processed.

This policy applies to all purchases made through our website vaibhavcelebrations.in, including celebration packages, physical products, personalized items, and digital deliverables.

1. General Principles

  • All refund requests must be made through our official support channels.
  • Submitting a refund request does not by itself create an entitlement to a refund. Eligibility is determined under this policy, the terms of your order and applicable law.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method used at checkout.
  • Processing time for refunds is 7-10 business days after approval.
  • Bank processing may take an additional 3-7 days for the amount to reflect in your account.
  • Refund eligibility depends on the type of purchase and timing of the request.

2. Deductions From Refunds

Where a refund is approved, we may deduct the following, wherever legally permissible:

  • Applicable payment gateway / transaction charges
  • Shipping charges
  • Customization and design charges
  • Procurement costs
  • Vendor cancellation charges
  • Other documented non-recoverable expenses

The refund amounts stated in this policy are subject to these deductions. No deduction is made where the refund arises from a damaged, defective or wrong item, or from a cancellation by Vaibhav Celebrations.

4. Failed Delivery and Return to Origin (RTO)

Where an order cannot be delivered and is returned to us for a reason attributable to the customer, the return shipping (RTO) charges and any re-dispatch charges apply and may be deducted from any refund. This includes:

  • An incorrect or incomplete address
  • The recipient being unavailable
  • Refusal to accept delivery
  • Repeated unsuccessful delivery attempts
  • A postponement of delivery requested by the customer
  • Failure to collect the order

7.2 Vendor Commitments

Once third-party vendors, materials, manpower or other event resources have been booked or committed for your event, their non-refundable or non-recoverable charges may be retained or deducted, to the extent legally permissible.

7.3 Rescheduling and Postponement by Customer

  • Free rescheduling: If requested more than 30 days before the event (subject to availability).
  • Rescheduling fee: May apply for requests within 30 days due to vendor rebooking costs.
  • Limit: One free reschedule per booking; subsequent changes may incur fees.
  • New date: Must be within 6 months of original date.
  • Postponement charges: Where an event is postponed at the customer's request or for a reason attributable to the customer, vendor rebooking charges, procurement charges and administrative charges may apply.

7.4 Cancellation by Vaibhav Celebrations

In rare cases where we must cancel (vendor unavailability, unforeseen circumstances):

  • Full refund will be provided, OR
  • Alternative arrangement of equal or greater value offered

7.5 Partial Execution

If the event is partially executed due to issues beyond our control:

  • Refund will be calculated proportionally based on services not delivered
  • Documented evidence of non-delivery is required

This does not apply where performance was prevented or affected for a reason attributable to the customer. See Sections 7.6 to 7.9.

7.6 Non-Performance Caused by the Customer

No automatic refund arises where performance is prevented or materially affected by the customer's failure to provide venue access, information, approvals, permissions, cooperation or other required assistance.

7.7 Event Delay

Where an event is delayed by the customer, the venue, traffic, weather, third parties or other circumstances outside our reasonable control, the delay is not attributable to Vaibhav Celebrations and does not by itself give rise to a refund. We will make reasonable efforts to perform the services within the time that remains available.

7.8 No-Show

Where the customer fails to attend the event, or fails to make the venue available at the agreed time, the booking is treated as a cancellation on the event date and no refund is due.

7.9 Customer Breach

Where cancellation or non-performance results from the customer's breach of the booking or service terms, the customer is not entitled to a refund of amounts already committed or incurred, and the cancellation charges in Section 7.1 and the deductions in Section 2 apply.

8. Force Majeure

If event execution is prevented by circumstances beyond reasonable control:

  • Natural disasters, pandemics, government restrictions
  • Venue closure, curfews, strikes
  • Extreme weather making execution unsafe

Resolution Options:

  • Rescheduling: To a mutually agreed date within 6 months
  • Credit: Full value as store credit valid for 12 months
  • Partial refund: Amount minus non-recoverable costs incurred

Each case is handled fairly with transparency about costs already committed.

9. Free Consultation / Enquiry

  • Submitting a consultation or enquiry form is free and does not create a paid booking.
  • No payment is collected; therefore, no refund is applicable.
  • You are under no obligation to proceed after a consultation.
