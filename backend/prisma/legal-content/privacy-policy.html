4. Children's Privacy Protection

Vaibhav Celebrations specializes in children's birthday celebrations and related services. We take the protection of children's data very seriously.

4.1 Our Commitments:

  • We do not allow children under 18 to create accounts, accept terms, or provide consent directly.
  • All child-related information (names, ages, photos) must be submitted by a parent, legal guardian, or authorized adult.
  • Child data is used solely for the requested celebration services and personalization.
  • We do not engage in behavioral advertising or profiling directed at children.
  • We do not send marketing communications to children.

4.2 Parental/Guardian Rights:

  • Parents/guardians may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's personal data at any time.
  • Contact our Grievance Officer (see Section 13) for such requests.
